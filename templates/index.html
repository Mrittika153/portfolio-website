About

I'm a Computer Science Engineering graduate with a solid grounding in C++, data structures & algorithms, object-oriented programming, SQL, and web technologies — and a growing focus on applied AI: LLMs, Retrieval-Augmented Generation, and the backend systems that make them actually usable.

My flagship project so far is an AI-powered question generator and online assessment system, built end-to-end with Flask, RAG, and ChromaDB. I like problems that sit at the intersection of backend engineering and applied AI — and when I'm not debugging one, I'm usually drawing, which might explain why this page is illustrated rather than stock photography.
